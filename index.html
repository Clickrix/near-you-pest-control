Our Story

Family owned. Community focused.

NEAR You Pest Control was originally founded as Rio Linda Pest Control by owner Dan Welch, who built the company on a simple belief: customers deserve honest service, reliable results, and a local company that truly cares.

What began as a one-man operation has grown into a family-owned and operated company serving Rio Linda, Elverta, Antelope, Natomas, Roseville, and surrounding areas. Before starting his own business, Dan worked in pest management and overseas helping provide sanitary, pest-free environments on military installations — experience that reinforced his attention to detail and high standards.
